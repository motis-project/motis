**`get_rule_walks` makes every transfers.txt row between two stops a minimum walk for all trips, the qualified rows too.** A qualified row should only bind the trips it names, and those trips already get their own rule footpaths through their virts. Everyone else should keep the normal walk.

The reviewers' probes show two effects:

1. **The walk gets slower for unrelated trips** (`qualified_rule_inflates_walk_of_other_routes`).
   - Row: `S,Y1,2,1800,RF,RG,,` (RF→RG changes from station S to stop Y1 take 30 min).
   - Trip P (another route) arrives at S1 and wants Q at Y1. The two stops are about 50 m apart, a 2-min walk.
   - Expected arrival 11:00, actual 11:30: the S1→Y1 walk became 30 min for everyone.
   - The loader reviewer found the same with `BS,BY1,2,600,RB,,,`.

2. **A walk appears that shouldn't exist** (`qualified_ban_creates_walk_for_other_routes`).
   - A route-qualified ban (type 3) between two stops 2 km apart, beyond `max_footpath_length`.
   - `get_rule_walks` records the pair, with 0 min for a ban. Rule-named pairs get a fill-in beeline walk that skips the `max_footpath_length` cap.
   - So the ban creates a ~25-min walk for all other trips, and P→Q becomes possible, though without the row there would be no walk at all.

For unqualified rows this behaviour is correct: the row is the stop pair's transfer for everyone, and may connect stops farther apart than the usual walk limit. The fix is to build `rule_walks` only from rows with no route or trip on either side.

The existing test `transfer_rules_virt_walk_respects_rule_pair` even says in its comment that the 2-min walk BP1→BY1 stays, which the code currently contradicts.

1. Confirmed bugs (most severe first)
merge_duplicates_keeps_rules_of_absorbed_trip. TA and TA2 are identical trips, and a timed TA2→TC transfer has a 1-minute gap. Expected 11:00, got no journey. merge() (merge_duplicates.cc:66-118) only repoints trip_transport_ranges_, so the survivor's transport_to_trip_section_ never lists TA2. get_state (virtual_locations.cc:215-226) therefore never sees TA2's rules. Bans are lost the same way. Fix: collect a transport's trips from trip_transport_ranges_, or don't merge transports that carry rules. Merging is opt-in; motis has it off by default.
majority_stop_default_vs_station_rule_at_virt. The fold gives stop X2 a 4-minute majority default and station X a 1-minute one. A change at X2 itself takes 4 minutes, but the same pair takes 1 minute when the departure sits at a virt. Cause: majority self-rules are stored only for stops with children (gtfs/transfers.cc:258), so get_most_specific picks X's rule for (X2, virt). Fix: always store majority rules.
qualified_rule_inflates_walk_of_other_routes. The row S,Y1,2,1800,RF,RG also stretches the S1→Y1 walk to 30 minutes for an unrelated P→Q change. Expected 11:00, got 11:30. Cause: get_rule_walks (build_footpaths.cc:38-66, applied at 539-549) takes the maximum over all rules, qualified ones included. The header calls this "not faster than the rule", but the comment in transfer_rules_virt_walk_respects_rule_pair says the 2-minute walk stays, so the two contradict. Fix: use only unqualified rules.
qualified_ban_creates_walk_for_other_routes. A qualified ban between two stops 2 km apart creates a walk that bypasses max_footpath_length; P→Q becomes possible. Cause: the fill-in walk for rule-named pairs at build_footpaths.cc:550-556.
u8_virt_transfer_time_clamped. A 300-minute RP1→RP2 rule: the two trips share one virt whose transfer time is clamped to 254 (to_transfer_time, footpath.h:85-89, called at transfer_rules.cc:49). A 260-minute gap therefore connects. find_conflicts compares rules, not times, so it doesn't notice. Fix: cap rule durations at 254 in the loader, or treat a self-applying side above 254 as unshareable.
rebuild_after_virts_keeps_rules. Virts are decided on the 2-minute beeline walk, then write_default_profile gets a 12-minute walk. Y has no rule of its own but shares F's virt, so it keeps F's 0-minute F→G timed rule and makes a 6-minute change. Root: the conflict check's fallback walk (virtual_locations.cc:341-343) is frozen at decision time. motis avoids this by deciding virts on the routed walks, but nigiri's own rebuild_default_profile_test exercises this path. Fix: only allow rebuilding before virts exist, or with the same walks.
